About Me

I am a Materials Science & Engineering graduate with research interests in electrochemical energy storage, advanced functional materials, and machine learning-assisted materials research. My research focuses on the development and investigation of electrode materials for lithium-ion batteries, with an emphasis on understanding the relationship between material structure, electrochemical behavior, and energy storage performance.

My research experience involves materials synthesis, structural and morphological characterization, and electrochemical performance evaluation using advanced techniques including X-ray Diffraction (XRD), Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy (FESEM), Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR), Cyclic Voltammetry (CV), Galvanostatic Charge-Discharge (GCD), and Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS).

I am particularly interested in exploring how material engineering strategies, defect chemistry, and data-driven approaches can contribute to the discovery and optimization of next-generation energy storage materials. By integrating experimental materials research with machine learning and scientific data analysis, I aim to develop a deeper understanding of advanced functional materials.

Through this platform, I share my research projects, scientific programming, data analysis work, and learning journey in materials science and sustainable energy technologies.
