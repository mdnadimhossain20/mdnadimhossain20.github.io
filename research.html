Research

My research interests focus on electrochemical energy storage, advanced functional materials, and machine learning-assisted materials research. I am particularly interested in understanding how material composition, structure, defects, and interfaces influence electrochemical behavior and overall device performance.

My research combines experimental materials development with advanced characterization techniques and data-driven approaches to accelerate the design and optimization of next-generation energy storage materials.

Current Research

Nitrogen-Phosphorus Co-Doped SnO₂ Anodes for Lithium-Ion Batteries

Undergraduate Thesis and Research Project
Department of Materials Science & Engineering, RUET
Duration: September 2024 – August 2026

This research focuses on the fabrication and investigation of Nitrogen-Phosphorus co-doped SnO₂ electrode materials to improve the electrochemical performance and cycling stability of lithium-ion batteries.

The study explores the role of doping-induced defect chemistry, structural modification, and surface/interface engineering in improving charge storage mechanisms and battery performance.

Research Themes

🔋 Lithium-ion Batteries

Development of advanced electrode materials for improved capacity, cycling stability, and energy storage performance.

🧪 Functional Materials

Design and modification of nanostructured materials for energy applications.

⚡ Electrochemical Systems

Investigation of charge transfer mechanisms and electrode-electrolyte interactions.

🤖 AI-Assisted Materials Research

Application of machine learning and data analysis for materials prediction and optimization.

Research Methodology

1. Materials Development

Synthesis and modification of electrode materials through controlled materials engineering strategies.

2. Structural & Morphological Characterization

Investigation of crystal structure, morphology, chemical bonding, and thermal properties using advanced characterization techniques.

3. Electrochemical Evaluation

Evaluation of battery performance through electrochemical measurements including charge-discharge behavior, cyclic voltammetry, and impedance analysis.

Research Techniques

Structural Characterization:
X-ray Diffraction (XRD), FESEM, FTIR

Electrochemical Analysis:
Cyclic Voltammetry (CV), Galvanostatic Charge-Discharge (GCD), Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS)

Data Analysis & Computational Tools:
Python, Machine Learning, MATLAB, OriginPro, Materials Studio, VESTA

Future Research Direction

My future research goal is to explore the integration of experimental materials science with computational modeling and machine learning approaches to accelerate the discovery of sustainable energy materials. I aim to contribute to the development of efficient batteries, fuel cells, and next-generation energy storage technologies.
